# YouTube-Channel-Performance-Analytics-portfolio-project
YouTube Channel Performance Analytics – Python, EDA Analyzed revenue, engagement, and retention metrics to identify monetization drivers and optimal content strategies.


Over All Business Impact--- Revenue on YouTube is not driven by views alone. Monetization efficiency (RPM/CPM), combined with strong engagement and retention, determines sustainable channel success. Mid-length videos provide the best balance between scale, audience retention, and revenue generation.

7–12 minutes is the optimal baseline duration.
Long-form videos should be used strategically for watch-time goals.

Engagement fuels algorithmic reach

Subscriber growth is closely tied to engagement quality

Focus on RPM, CPM, and audience quality, not just view growth

Analyze why some low-view videos earn more than high-view ones

Segment videos by monetization efficiency

Use median revenue per video as a KPI

Identify and replicate characteristics of high-revenue outliers

Avoid assuming “average revenue” reflects typical performance
